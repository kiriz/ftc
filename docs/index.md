This site explains FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) and this season's challenge, BIOBUZZ (2026–27), for the families, students and coaches of the Leander FTC Team. We are a new, parent-led middle school team in Leander, Texas. This is not an official FIRST site.

## Start here

- [I'm a parent](parents.md): what joining involves, costs and how to register your student.
- [I'm a student](students.md): team roles, what you will do, and how to learn programming.
- [I'm a coach or mentor](coaches.md): roles, the registration checklist and what to keep on record.

New to FTC? Read [What is FTC](about-ftc.md) first.
