## The program

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a robotics program for students in grades 7–12 (ages 12–18). Each season, FIRST releases a new game. Teams design, build and program a robot to play that game.

- **Team size:** up to 15 students. There is no official minimum, but at least 2 students are needed to drive the robot in a match.
- **Alliance:** two teams that play together in a match. Each match is two alliances against each other, so four robots are on the field.
- **Match:** about 2.5 minutes. The first 30 seconds are **autonomous**, meaning the robot runs code the students wrote, with no one driving. The last 2 minutes are **driver-controlled**, meaning students drive the robot with game controllers.
- **Judged awards:** at a league tournament, judges review each team's work and give awards. The top award is the **Inspire Award**.
- **Engineering portfolio:** a short document the team writes about its robot, its design process and its outreach. Judges read it.
- **Gracious Professionalism:** a core FIRST value. It means competing hard while treating other teams with respect and helping them when you can.

## How FTC is organized in Texas

- **FIRST** runs the program worldwide.
  - **FIRST Texas** runs it in Texas.
    - **Five regions:** North, Central, South, East, and West and Panhandle (one region).
      - **Areas** within each region (for example, the Austin area in Central Texas).
        - **Leagues** within each area. Larger leagues are split into **divisions**.

**Central** Texas Texas includes the Austin area (Travis, Williamson, Hays, Caldwell, Bastrop and Bell counties), San Antonio, Corpus Christi and Waco.
