## Roles

| Role | Required? | What they can do | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead Coach 1 | Yes | Leads the team | Age 21 or older; FIRST consent form, youth protection training and screening |
| Lead Coach 2 | Yes | Same as Lead Coach 1 | Same as Lead Coach 1 |
| Team Administrator | No | Full admin rights; can invite or remove Lead Coaches | Youth protection clearance if they manage the youth roster |
| Mentor | No | Help students at practices and events | Follow team and FIRST rules |

Lead Coaches do not need to be parents of students on the team.

## Registration checklist

1. Both Lead Coaches [create a FIRST account](https://my.firstinspires.org/AccountManager/Account/Register).
2. Both Lead Coaches complete the consent form, youth protection training and screening in the [FIRST Dashboard](https://my.firstinspires.org/Dashboard/).
3. Register the team and pay the $350 FIRST season registration by Nov 15, 2026.
4. Ask the Central Texas program administrator for league placement. Find the contact on the [FIRST Texas FTC regions and contacts page](https://www.firsttx.org/ftc-regions).
5. Pay the $325 Texas league fee by Dec 15, 2026. See [FIRST Texas FTC pricing and registration](https://www.firsttx.org/ftc-registration).
6. Make sure every student has a FIRST account, is on the roster, and has a signed Consent and Release Form.
7. Order robot parts. See [Links](links.md) for vendors.

## Who to contact for league placement

The Central Texas program administrator places teams in leagues. Contact details are on the [FIRST Texas FTC regions and contacts page](https://www.firsttx.org/ftc-regions). For general questions, use the same page or the [FIRST Help Center](https://help.firstinspires.org/).

## Fixed versus team decides

| Fixed by FIRST or FIRST Texas | The team decides |
|---|---|
| Game rules and the Competition Manual | Practice schedule and location |
| Registration and league fees and due dates | How costs are split between families |
| League meet and league tournament dates | Student roles |
| Lead Coach requirements | Robot design and programming tool |
| Maximum 15 students; grades 7–12 | Outreach activities |

## What to record and keep

- Proof of payment for the FIRST registration and the Texas league fee.
- Receipts for robot parts and other spending.
- The team roster and each student's Consent and Release Form status.
- Lead Coach youth protection and screening status.
- Engineering notes and photos for the engineering portfolio.
