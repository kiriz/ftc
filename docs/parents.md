## What joining involves

Your student joins a team of up to 15 students in grades 7–12. Over the season, the team designs, builds and programs a robot, writes an engineering portfolio, and competes at league meets and a league tournament. You do not need any robotics background to help.

## How to register your student

Each student needs a FIRST account, must be on the team roster, and needs a FIRST Consent and Release Form signed by a parent or guardian.

1. Read the [Youth Registration guide for families](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/youth-registration).
2. [Create a FIRST account](https://my.firstinspires.org/AccountManager/Account/Register) for your student, following that guide.
3. Ask a Lead Coach to add your student to the team roster.
4. Sign the FIRST Consent and Release Form in the [FIRST Dashboard](https://my.firstinspires.org/Dashboard/).
5. If you get stuck, search the [FIRST Help Center](https://help.firstinspires.org/).

## How parents can help

- Drive students to practices and events.
- Help at practices, even without a technical background.
- Help find grants and keep track of team spending.
- Become a mentor or a Lead Coach. See [For coaches and mentors](coaches.md).
