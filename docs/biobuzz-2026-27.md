BIOBUZZ presented by RTX is the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) game for the 2026–27 season, part of the FIRST CANOPY season. Kickoff was September 12, 2026.

## The game in plain words

The game pieces are called **pollen** and **nectar**.

1. Robots launch pieces into their alliance's **hive**.
2. When enough pieces are in the hive, it tips. That scores points and releases more nectar.
3. Near the end of the match, robots place nectar and pollen into **flowers**. Points go to the first alliance to place nectar in each flower, and to the alliance whose nectar is on top.

Teams can also earn extra **ranking points** for reaching certain goals. Ranking points decide a team's place in the standings.

## Match periods

| Period | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Autonomous | First 30 seconds | The robot runs the students' code with no driver |
| Driver-controlled | Last 2 minutes | Students drive the robot with game controllers |
| Whole match | About 2.5 minutes | Two alliances of two teams each |

1. Watch the [BIOBUZZ game animation video](https://youtu.be/sUH3z5a5S9I).
2. Watch the [BIOBUZZ field walkthrough video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47X9sYnPijw).
3. Read the [BIOBUZZ gameplay card (PDF)](https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/program/ftc/biobuzz-gamepostcard.pdf).
4. Read the [Competition Manual](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual). This is the official rule book.

## New this season

- Section 1 of the Competition Manual includes a **Competition Integrity Contract**. The whole team should review it together.
- A robot may use **at most 8 servos** (small motors that turn to a set position).
- The limit on which game controllers are allowed has been **removed**.
- The judging interview is now called the **Initial Interview**. It may be in person or remote, depending on the event.
