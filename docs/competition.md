## League meets versus the league tournament

| | League meet | League tournament |
|---|---|---|
| Matches | Yes (about 5 per meet last season) | Yes |
| Judging | No | Yes: engineering portfolio, interview and observation |
| Awards | No | Yes |
| Alliance selection and playoffs | No | Yes |
| Advancement to the next event | No | Yes |
| Results | Count toward ranking at the league tournament | Decide who advances |

Our league season (estimated, XSTREAM league) has 3 league meets and 1 league tournament.

## Ranking versus judging

- **Ranking** comes from match results. Ranking points are earned in matches, including extra ranking points for certain game goals.
- **Judging** looks at the team's work: the engineering portfolio, the Initial Interview with judges, and what judges observe at the event. Judged awards are given at the league tournament. The top award is the **Inspire Award**.

## Advancement points

At the league tournament, teams earn **advancement points** from:

1. Qualification ranking.
2. Alliance selection.
3. Playoff result.
4. Judged awards.

Teams are compared only with other teams at the same event.

## The path to the Texas State Cup

1. League meets (3), then the league tournament.
2. Austin Semi-Regional (if we advance).
3. Central Texas Regional Championship (if we advance).
4. Texas State Cup (by invitation).
5. FIRST Championship in Houston (by invitation).

For the exact advancement rules this season, see [FIRST Texas FTC advancement](https://www.firsttx.org/ftc-advancement).

The UIL State Championship is only for teams that represent a high school, so it does not apply to our team.

## How hard each step was last season (2025–26)

| Step | Teams at the event | Teams that moved up |
|---|---|---|
| Each Austin league tournament | about 36 | about 5–6 |
| Austin Semi-Regional | 32 | 4 |
| Central Texas Regional Championship | 48 | To be announced |

## Our plan to advance

1. Have a working robot that can score in both the autonomous and driver-controlled periods by the first league meet.
2. Use the 3 league meets to build our ranking and improve the robot between meets.
3. Keep engineering notes all season so the engineering portfolio is ready for the league tournament.
4. Practice the Initial Interview before the league tournament.
