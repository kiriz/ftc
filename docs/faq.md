## Frequently asked questions

### Do we need a school?

No. Our team is parent-led. A school matters only for the UIL State Championship, which is for teams that represent a high school. See [Cost and Registration](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/cost-and-registration) for team requirements.

### What if we don't get a league?

League placement is not yet confirmed. The Central Texas program administrator places teams in leagues. Contact details are on the [FIRST Texas FTC regions and contacts page](https://www.firsttx.org/ftc-regions). Until placement is confirmed, our calendar assumes the XSTREAM league.

### Are league meets judged?

No. League meets have matches only: no judging, no awards, no playoffs and no advancement. Results count toward ranking at the league tournament. Judging happens at the league tournament.

### Can a parent who isn't a coach help?

Yes. Parents can help as mentors, drive students, help at practices and help find grants. See [How parents can help](parents.md#how-parents-can-help).

### What does it cost?

About $2,175 for a typical league season, before sales tax: $350 FIRST registration, $325 Texas league fee and about $1,500 in robot parts. More fees apply only if the team advances. See [Costs](parents.md#costs).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Advancement points | Points earned at the league tournament from ranking, alliance selection, playoffs and judged awards. They decide which teams move up. |
| Alliance | Two teams that play together in a match. |
| Autonomous | The first 30 seconds of a match, when the robot runs the students' code with no driver. |
| Control Hub | The robot's main computer, made by REV Robotics. |
| Division | A part of a larger league. For example, XSTREAM has A and B divisions. |
| Driver-controlled | The last 2 minutes of a match, when students drive the robot. |
| Driver Station | The device and game controllers the drive team uses to control the robot. |
| Engineering portfolio | A short document about the team's robot, design process and outreach. Judges read it. |
| Inspire Award | The top judged award. |
| League | A group of nearby teams that compete together during the season. |
| League meet | A league event with matches only. No judging or awards. |
| League tournament | The final league event, with matches, judging, playoffs, awards and advancement. |
| Lead Coach | One of two required adult team leaders, each 21 or older and cleared by FIRST. |
| Qualifier | An event where teams can earn a place at the next level of competition. In our league, the league tournament is where advancement happens. |
| Ranking points | Points earned in matches that decide a team's place in the standings. |
| Regional Championship | The Central Texas competition after the league tournament and Semi-Regional. |
| Semi-Regional | An event between the league tournament and the Regional Championship. |
| StarterBot | A simple robot design from FIRST that new teams can build. |
| State Cup | The Texas State Cup, the top FIRST Texas event, by invitation. |
| Team Administrator | An optional adult role with full admin rights that can invite or remove Lead Coaches. |
